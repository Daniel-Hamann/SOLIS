A Design for a Viable Social Order

The Path to the
Future

SOLIS

None of our existing orders can carry the future any longer. This is the design for one that could: resting on five ethical foundations, carried by institutions that remain just even with fallible people.

Translated from the German original with the help of artificial intelligence. Corrections are welcome on GitHub: github.com/Daniel-Hamann/SOLIS

01

Human Dignity

The human being as a value in themselves: inviolable, regardless of origin, achievement, or belief.

02

Balance between Individual and Community

Freedom to grow and community to flourish, held in a regulated tension.

03

Responsibility for the Planet

The Earth as the living world on which every society depends, preserved and restored.

04

Advancement of Knowledge and Wisdom

Decisions based on the best available knowledge, joined with ethical judgment.

05

Long-Term Resilience

An order that emerges stronger from strain: able to learn, decentralized, adaptable.
